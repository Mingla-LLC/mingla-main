Decision 01

How hard do we push the bank?

A partner without a bank cannot take a single payment. But a hard wall on a page they reached from an email is also how you lose someone who just wanted to look.

Hard gate — no escape Bank or nothing. Highest conversion for those who finish; highest abandon.
RecommendedQuiet text link, below the fold of the thumb One loud CTA, one whispered “I'll add it later” in tertiary text. It exists, it is reachable, it never competes. Skipping routes through a confirm sheet that states the actual cost.
Equal secondary button What we ship today, one step later. Two buttons of similar weight is exactly the leak #948 was filed to close.

Affects C1 · D4 · D5

Decision 02

Bank before the app download?

This makes a bank form the partner's first real Mingla experience. No product tour, no dopamine — a form asking for account numbers.

RecommendedYes — bank first, app immediately after They arrived from an email that already promised “claim + get paid”, so the bank is the expected next beat, not a surprise. Crucially there is no deferred deep-link continuity: install-before-accept strands the invite entirely, so the app genuinely cannot come first.
App first, bank inside the app Warmer first impression, but hands the partner to a store listing mid-funnel and the invite context dies at the store door.

Decision 03

Nigeria vs Stripe inside one CTA

One button, two completely different destinations: a Stripe hosted flow, or a Mingla-owned Nigerian bank form. Today the partner picks a country from a list to get there.

RecommendedPre-resolve from brands.country_code, show a confirm row The invite was created by us — we already know where the brand is. Show “Payouts in Nigeria · NGN” with a quiet Change. Zero picker taps for the 99% case.
Keep the country picker as the first screen Honest but slow, and it presents an ex-ticketing 34-country list to a Lagos restaurant.
