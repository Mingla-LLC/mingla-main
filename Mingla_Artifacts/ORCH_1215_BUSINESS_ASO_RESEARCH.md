**Read of the field:**
- The closest analogues (Eventbrite Organizer, Universe BoxOffice, DICE Access, Ticket Tailor, SimpleTix Organizer, Prekindle) cluster hard in **Business** category and lead their subtitle with the door/scan/sell job: *"Scan, manage attendees & sell"*, *"Manage ticketing at your event"*, *"Event check in and management."*
- Consumer-discovery apps (Posh, Luma, Fever, DICE: Live Shows) sit in **Entertainment / Lifestyle** and use vibe taglines (*"Find your world."*). Mingla Business is an **operator tool**, so it belongs with the first cluster, not the second.
- Payments/POS incumbents (Square, Toast) own the **Business** category with payments-first subtitles (*"Payments & Business Dashboard"*). Mingla Business's payments + payouts story competes adjacent to these.

## 2. Naming conventions

**Dominant pattern:** `Brand` + (delimiter) + `keyword tail describing the operator job`.

- **Delimiter usage:** Apple/Play titles use either a colon (`Luma: Events & Invites`, `Fever: Events & Tickets`), an en-dash (`Posh – Create & Find Events`, `Square - Restaurants POS`), or a trailing role word (`Eventbrite Organizer`, `DICE Access`, `SevenRooms OS`, `OpenTable for Restaurants`).
- **Operator-signal tails that recur:** `Organizer`, `BoxOffice` / `Box Office`, `Access`, `Event Entry`, `Guest List`, `Check In`, `OS`, `for Restaurants`, `for Artists`, `POS`. These tell the algorithm *and the buyer* "this is the run-the-event/run-the-venue app," distinguishing it from the consumer app.
- **Recurring keyword phrases across titles+subtitles:** "sell tickets" / "sell" (Eventbrite, Universe, Bandsintown), "check-in" (Eventbrite, Guestlist, Universe, OpenTable), "manage events / event management" (Eventbrite, SimpleTix, Guestlist), "scan" (Universe, DICE Access), "at the door" (Universe, Ticket Tailor).

**Character budgets (the hard constraints):**

| Field | Apple | Google Play |
|---|---|---|
| App name / title | **30 chars** | **30 chars** |
| Subtitle (Apple) / Short description (Play) | **30 chars** | **80 chars** |
| Keyword field | **100 chars** (hidden) | *none* (Play has no keyword field; it indexes the full long description) |

Sources: [Apple — Creating Your Product Page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/); [iOS keyword field rules](https://www.appalize.com/blog/keyword-research/ios-keyword-field-rules-character-limits-optimization); [App Store keyword field 100-char limit](https://asopulse.com/blog/app-store-keyword-field-100-characters-limit).

**Recommended name candidates for Mingla Business (≤30 chars):**

| Candidate | Chars | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `Mingla Business` | 15 | Clean brand-only; wastes ~15 chars of indexable name real estate. |
| `Mingla: Sell Event Tickets` | 27 | Puts the #1 high-intent phrase *in the name* (Apple weights name >> keyword field). |
| `Mingla: Sell Tickets & Events` | 29 | Two head terms in the name. |
| `Mingla Business: Sell Tickets` | 29 | Keeps "Business" (brand consistency + category signal) + head term. |
| `Mingla: Events & Box Office` | 27 | "Box Office" = strong operator-search tail (per Universe). |

> **Recommendation:** lead the **name** with a head keyword — `Mingla: Sell Tickets & Events` (29) on iOS — because Apple ranks the *title* far above the keyword field. Keep `Mingla Business` as the developer-facing brand and in the icon. (Confirm against any existing live listing — current Mingla Business store name is **unverified** here.)

**Subtitle (Apple, 30 chars) — pick one job-to-be-done, not a feature dump:**
- `Tickets, payments & door scan` (29)
- `Sell tickets, scan at the door` (30)
- `Run events, payments & guests` (29)
- `Events, tickets & guest lists` (29)

**Play short description (80 chars) — wider, so stack high-intent terms naturally:**
- `Sell event tickets, take payments, scan guests at the door & run guest lists.` (76)
- `Event ticketing, payments & payouts, QR check-in, guest lists & marketing.` (73)

| Store | Primary | Secondary | Why |

| **Apple App Store** | **Business** | Lifestyle *(optional)* | Every operator-side analogue sits in **Business**: Eventbrite Organizer, DICE Access, Ticket Tailor, SimpleTix Organizer, Prekindle Event Entry, Guestlist, Square POS, Square Restaurants POS. Mingla Business is a B2B operator tool (tickets, payments, payouts, scanning, analytics) — it belongs here. *Caveat:* Universe BoxOffice chose **Entertainment** and Luma chose **Lifestyle**, but those skew consumer-facing; Mingla Business is operator-first, so Business is the better fit. |
| **Google Play** | **Business** | *(Play allows only one category)* | Same logic. Toast Now, Square Restaurants, Eventbrite Organizer all live in Play's business/productivity space. **Do NOT choose Food & Drink** despite the restaurant audience, and **do not choose Entertainment/Lifestyle** — those are consumer-discovery categories where Mingla Business's payment/ticketing/door tooling would be mis-shelved against buyer apps. Business is where venue owners and organizers browse for operator software. (Apple lets you set a secondary category; Lifestyle is the only mildly defensible secondary, but Business-only is cleaner.)

## 5. Apple ASC keyword field (100 chars) — guidance + draft

**Rules (verified):** ([Apple product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) · [keyword field rules](https://www.appalize.com/blog/keyword-research/ios-keyword-field-rules-character-limits-optimization) · [100-char limit](https://asopulse.com/blog/app-store-keyword-field-100-characters-limit))

1. **100 characters total**, comma-separated, **no space after commas** (a space wastes a character and Apple still tokenizes on commas). Use spaces only *inside* a multi-word phrase if you intend that exact phrase, but Apple auto-combines single words across the keyword field + title, so prefer **single words** to maximize combinations.
2. **Do not repeat** any word already in the **app name** or **subtitle** — those are already indexed; repeating wastes characters.
3. **Singular only** — Apple matches singular↔plural automatically. Use `ticket` not `tickets` (the head phrase `sell tickets` can live in the *name/subtitle* instead).
4. **No competitor brand names** (Eventbrite, Posh, DICE, Square, Toast, etc.) — disallowed, risks rejection.
5. **No "app", "free", category names, or your own brand** — wasted; Apple already knows.
6. **Fill all 100 characters** — every unused char is lost discovery.

**Draft keyword field** (assumes the name/subtitle already carry: *sell, tickets, events, payments, door, scan* — so those words are intentionally omitted here to avoid duplication). Singular, no spaces after commas:

```
ticketing,scanner,checkin,guest,list,boxoffice,organizer,venue,host,qr,rsvp,payout,marketing,analytics,experience,booking,nightlife,attendee
```
Count this against 100 chars when the final name/subtitle is locked and **trim duplicates** of whatever ends up in the visible fields. (The draft above is ~135 chars as written — it is a *candidate pool*; select the ~100-char highest-value subset after the name/subtitle copy is final.)

**Trimmed 100-char-safe example** (if name = `Mingla: Sell Tickets & Events`, subtitle = `Tickets, payments & door scan`):
```
ticketing,scanner,checkin,guest,list,boxoffice,organizer,venue,host,qr,rsvp,payout,marketing,attendee
```
(101 chars — drop `attendee` or `rsvp` to land ≤100.)

> Because Play has **no keyword field**, replicate this keyword set *naturally inside the Play long description* (Play indexes the full description). Repeat each head term 2–4× across the description without keyword-stuffing.
