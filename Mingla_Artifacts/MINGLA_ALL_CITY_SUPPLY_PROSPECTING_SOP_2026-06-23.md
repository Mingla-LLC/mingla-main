# Mingla All-City Supply Prospecting SOP

Date: 2026-06-23
Owner: Seth / Sales Support
System of record: ClickUp `Mingla Growth` -> `02 Supply Pipeline`
Primary lists: `Supply CRM`, `Activity Log`, `Creator Onboarding`

## 1. Purpose

This SOP turns city prospecting into a repeatable operating system. The goal is to find high-fit Mingla supply candidates, qualify them quickly, contact them with a specific first ask, track every next action in ClickUp, and move accepted leads into onboarding without losing momentum.

The first city lanes are:

- Triangle: Raleigh, Durham, Cary, Chapel Hill, and nearby owner-operated hospitality pockets.
- DC: restaurants, bars, nightlife, cultural programming, recurring organizers, and food/event partners.
- Lagos: lounges, nightlife promoters, creator-led events, beach clubs, event centers, ticketed experiences, and WhatsApp/DM-heavy sellers.

The same workflow applies to every future city.

## 2. Operating Principle

Do not prospect from memory, screenshots, or loose spreadsheets.

Every qualified lead must become a `Supply CRM` card with:

- City
- Segment
- Source URL
- Pain signal
- Personalization token
- First ask
- Lead score
- Next action date
- Next action type

A temporary spreadsheet or AI tool can stage and enrich leads, but ClickUp is the source of truth once a lead is qualified.

## 4. Weekly City Rhythm

### Monday: Pick The Wedge

Choose one primary city, one secondary city, one segment, one offer, and one bottleneck.

Example:

- Primary city: DC
- Segment: RAMW restaurants/bars with event or Restaurant Week moments
- Offer: mirror one event or recurring night as a clean Mingla page
- Bottleneck: first replies from owners/managers
- Proof target: 2 live pages or 5 useful objections

Monday quotas:

- Add 25-30 qualified `Supply CRM` prospects for the primary city.
- Add 10-15 qualified prospects for the secondary city.
- Confirm every A-tier prospect has a next action date.
- Draft the week's first-touch message and follow-up variant.

### Tuesday To Thursday: Execute

Daily quotas:

- 20-35 outbound touches across DM, email, phone, LinkedIn, field visit, or WhatsApp.
- 5-10 personalized follow-ups.
- 3-5 asset asks, setup asks, or call asks.
- 1 proof artifact captured if possible.
- 1 `Activity Log` card completed before end of day.

### Friday: Review And Decide

Review by city, segment, source, channel, and message.

Friday questions:

- Which source produced the most A-tier leads?
- Which first ask got replies?
- Which objection repeated?
- Which leads moved from `contacted` to `meeting set`, `onboarded`, `live`, or `active`?
- Which city/segment deserves another week?
- Which city/segment should be parked?

Friday output:

- Update the SOP notes if a city-specific source or objection matters.
- Convert the best objection into a response asset.
- Pick next week's wedge.

## 5. Daily SOP

### Step 1: Choose The City Lane

Pick only one primary lane per work block.

| Lane | When to choose it | Best first ask |
|---|---|---|
| Triangle restaurants/bars/cafes | You want fast founder access, field visits, owner-operated conversations, and local proof | "Send one menu item, flyer, or recurring night. We will turn it into a shareable Mingla page." |
| Triangle programming venues | You see trivia, brunch, live music, workshops, markets, or community nights | "Let's turn one recurring night into a repeatable audience loop." |
| DC restaurants/bars | They have Restaurant Week, brunch, cultural programming, nightlife, or event moments | "Let us mirror one event or recurring night so people can discover, share, buy/book, and follow up from it." |
| DC organizers/promoters | They run recurring parties, cultural events, diaspora events, workshops, or venue nights | "Give us one event and one flyer. We will turn it into a page, checkout, and share plan." |
| Lagos nightlife/promoters | Selling happens through WhatsApp, DMs, flyers, Tix Africa, Popout, Eventbrite, or manual lists | "Keep WhatsApp for conversation. Use Mingla for the page, payment, ticket, guest list, and proof." |
| Lagos lounges/beach clubs/event centers | They have high-vibe spaces, creator nights, brunches, launch parties, or ticketed moments | "Send one flyer and we will turn it into a cleaner page/checkout test." |

### Step 2: Source Leads

Use public, legitimate sources. Do not scrape private data or bypass platform rules.

Universal source types:

- Restaurant association/member lists
- Chamber of commerce directories
- Business improvement district directories
- Downtown association directories
- Event platforms
- Instagram venue calendars
- Instagram promoter pages
- Venue websites
- Google Maps categories
- Local press opening lists
- Food/event newsletters
- Creator pages
- Referral lists
- Field walk notes

City-specific starting points:

| City | Source lanes |
|---|---|
| Triangle | Downtown Raleigh Alliance, Raleigh Chamber, Downtown Durham Inc., Durham Chamber, Cary Chamber, Shop Local Raleigh, local Instagram event calendars, food/opening lists |
| DC | RAMW restaurants/bars, Restaurant Week participants, Washington.org event listings, Events DC, neighborhood BIDs, nightlife venues, cultural/diaspora event calendars |
| Lagos | Tix Africa, Popout Tickets, Eventbrite Lagos nightlife, Lagos event Instagram pages, Landmark-style venue/event listings, beach clubs, lounges, creator/promoter pages |

Lead capture rule:

- Capture the source URL every time.
- If there is no URL, write the field note and where the lead came from.
- If there is no upcoming moment, only keep the lead if the place has unusually strong vibe, owner access, or referral value.

### Step 3: Enrich And Dedupe

For every lead, collect:

- Business / creator / promoter name
- City
- Segment
- Website URL
- Instagram URL
- Event/listing URL
- Contact name, if public
- Contact role, if public
- Current tool or workaround
- Upcoming moment
- Pain signal
- Personalization token
- First ask

Dedupe rules:

- Same Instagram profile = same lead.
- Same website domain = same lead.
- Same venue with multiple events = one venue card plus event notes, unless a different promoter owns the event relationship.
- Same promoter across multiple venues = one promoter card plus linked venue notes.

### Step 4: Score Leads

Use a 10-point score.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---:|
| Upcoming event, recurring programming, campaign, launch, or offer in next 30 days | +3 |
| Visible payment or booking friction: DMs, WhatsApp, Google Forms, flyer-only, scattered links | +2 |
| Strong vibe but weak public page/link/checkout | +2 |
| Active social presence | +1 |
| Owner-operated, creator-led, or promoter-led | +1 |
| In priority city wedge | +1 |
| Enterprise chain or procurement-heavy | -3 |
| No upcoming moment | -2 |
| No clear contact path | -2 |

Priority:

- A-tier, 8-10: contact now.
- B-tier, 5-7: contact after A-tier or batch into nurture.
- C-tier, 0-4: park unless there is a warm intro.

Create one `Supply CRM` card per qualified lead.

Card title format:

`[City] [Segment] - [Lead Name] - [Upcoming Moment or Pain Signal]`

Examples:

- `[DC] Restaurant - Example Bistro - Restaurant Week page`
- `[Lagos] Promoter - Example Nights - WhatsApp ticketing`
- `[Triangle] Bar - Example Lounge - Thursday slow-night series`

Card description template:

```text
Lead:
City:
Segment:
Source URL:
Website:
Instagram:
Contact:
Current workaround:
Upcoming moment:
Pain signal:
Personalization token:
First ask:
Lead score:
Suggested channel:
Next action:

```

Status rules:

| ClickUp status | Trigger |
|---|---|
| `to do` | Raw imported lead, not scored yet |
| `prospect` | Scored and approved for outreach |
| `contacted` | First touch sent |
| `meeting set` | Call, visit, or async setup agreed |
| `onboarded` | Prospect says yes or sends assets; Creator Onboarding card created |
| `live` | First page, event, link, checkout, campaign, or QR is live |
| `active` | Repeat publish, repeat share, buyer/lead proof, or ongoing partner behavior |
| `at risk` | No next action, stalled after yes, not sharing, or no response after meaningful engagement |
| `churned` | Explicit no, wrong fit, or follow-up sequence complete with no response |

Non-negotiable rule:

- No `Supply CRM` card is complete without a next action date.

### Step 6: Run Outreach

Use the shortest channel that matches the operator's world.

| Segment | Best channels |
|---|---|
| Restaurants/bars/cafes | Instagram DM, email, phone, in-person visit |
| Venues/nightlife | Instagram DM, phone, promoter intro, field visit |
| Recurring organizers/promoters | Instagram DM, WhatsApp where appropriate, email, warm intro |
| DC restaurant/business partners | Email, LinkedIn, association intro, phone |
| Lagos promoters/venues | WhatsApp where publicly shared, Instagram DM, event platform contact, warm intro |
| Creators | Instagram DM, TikTok/LinkedIn where relevant, warm intro |

Five-touch sequence:

1. Pain recognition: name the moment and the broken workaround.
2. Specific offer: ask for one flyer, event, menu, or recurring night.
3. Proof or founder field note: show a before/after, page mock, or honest observation.
4. Low-friction ask: ask for 15-20 minutes or permission to draft one page.
5. Graceful close: stop chasing, leave the door open around their real pain.

First-touch structure:

```text
Saw [specific moment/source]. Looks like [business/creator] is already doing the hard part: creating a reason for people to show up.

The gap I noticed is [pain signal: flyer-only / DM checkout / weak link / event buried on Instagram / no clean page].

Mingla helps turn that into a page people can discover, share, buy/book from, and follow up from.

If you send me [one flyer/menu/event], I can show you what it would look like as a Mingla page. Worth trying on [specific upcoming moment]?
```

### Step 7: Handle Replies

Reply categories:

| Reply | Action |
|---|---|
| Interested | Move to `meeting set` or request assets; set next action within 24 hours |
| Send info | Send one relevant example, not a long deck; set follow-up |
| Too busy | Offer founder-assisted setup from one flyer/menu/event |
| Already use another tool | Ask where the process still breaks: promo, checkout, door, repeat buyers, or follow-up |
| Need demand proof | Do not promise attendance; propose a controlled share/link/checkout test |
| No | Mark reason, move to `churned` or future nurture |
| No response | Continue sequence until touch 5, then park or churn |

Every meaningful reply gets:

- Status update
- Next action date
- Objection or learning
- Follow-up step

### Step 8: Move Yeses Into Creator Onboarding

When a prospect says yes, sends an asset, or agrees to a test:

1. Create a `Creator Onboarding` card.
2. Link it in the `Supply CRM` card.
3. Move the `Supply CRM` card to `onboarded`.
4. Put the accepted asset or event details in the onboarding card.
5. Assign the next fulfillment owner.
6. Set an expected publish date.

Creator Onboarding card title format:

`[City] [Lead Name] - [Asset/Event] - Page setup`

Onboarding entry checklist:

- Asset received
- Copy needed?
- Design/cover needed?
- Ticket/booking/payment needed?
- Public page needed?
- Approval owner
- Promo date
- Live page URL
- Post-event follow-up date

### Step 9: Log Daily Activity

Create one `Activity Log` card per person per day.

Activity Log minimum fields:

- Date
- City
- Segment
- Leads sourced
- Leads scored
- DMs sent
- Emails sent
- Calls made
- Field visits
- Replies
- Meetings set
- Assets received
- Pages live
- Objections heard
- Best learning
- Tomorrow's focus

This prevents the team from confusing a full CRM with real motion.

### Step 10: Capture Proof

Proof beats theory. Capture at least one proof item per week.

Proof types:

- Before/after page
- Live Mingla page URL
- QR/link placed in bio, story, flyer, or venue
- First share
- First click/buyer/lead
- Operator reply
- Objection screenshot or summary
- Field note from visit
- Partner quote, only if permission is clear
- Product gap discovered during setup

Do not invent proof. If proof is thin, say the company is in proof-building mode and ask for a controlled test.

## 6. City Playbooks

### Triangle SOP

Best target:

- Independent restaurants, bars, cafes, lounges, breweries, community venues, programming-heavy spots, owner-operated story-rich places.

Priority neighborhoods/pockets:

- Downtown Raleigh
- Glenwood South
- Durham downtown
- Cary downtown
- Chapel Hill / Carrboro
- Food halls, markets, college-adjacent programming, local creator pop-ups

Best source pattern:

1. Pull directory/listing leads from downtown/chamber/local business sources.
2. Cross-check Instagram for recent events, menu drops, recurring programming, and vibe.
3. Prioritize owner-operated venues with public-facing personality.
4. Use field visits for A-tier leads.

Best pain signals:

- Great vibe but weak link
- Event only exists as an Instagram post
- Slow-night programming
- Menu/story not packaged
- Community night without repeat loop
- No clear buyer/lead follow-up

Best first ask:

```text
Send me one menu item, flyer, or recurring night. I will turn it into a clean Mingla page and share link so we can see if people respond.
```

Triangle weekly target:

- 30 new A/B leads
- 75 touches
- 5 replies
- 2 setup conversations
- 1 live page or page draft

### DC SOP

Best target:

- RAMW restaurants/bars, Restaurant Week participants, nightlife venues, cultural/community event organizers, recurring promoters, diaspora events, venue-based programming.

Priority pockets:

- RAMW member and Restaurant Week lists
- Shaw / U Street / Adams Morgan / H Street / Navy Yard / downtown corridors
- Cultural venues and event calendars
- Food/drink events with ticketed or RSVP moments

Best source pattern:

1. Start with RAMW and Restaurant Week style lists.
2. Identify upcoming events, prix fixe weeks, brunches, tastings, live music, cultural nights, and private-room activations.
3. Cross-check Instagram and website path.
4. Score highest when the event exists but the conversion path is scattered.

Best pain signals:

- Event buried on a website calendar
- Ticket/RSVP path separated from marketing post
- Restaurant Week attention not captured into repeat audience
- Strong venue calendar but weak shareability
- Promoter owns demand but venue owns operations

Best first ask:

```text
You already have the demand moment. Let us mirror one event or recurring night as a Mingla page people can discover, share, buy/book from, and follow up from.
```

DC weekly target:

- 30 new A/B leads
- 75 touches
- 4 replies
- 2 meetings or async setup conversations
- 1 mirrored event/page test

### Lagos SOP

Best target:

- Nightlife promoters, lounges, beach clubs, event centers, creator-led events, ticketed food/drink/culture experiences, pop-ups, WhatsApp/DM-heavy sellers.

Priority pockets:

- Victoria Island
- Lekki
- Ikoyi
- Landmark/beach club style venues
- Creator/promoter accounts with recurring event flyers
- Tix Africa / Popout / Eventbrite event pages

Best source pattern:

1. Start with event platforms and Instagram event calendars.
2. Identify promoters and venues repeatedly publishing flyers.
3. Cross-check whether checkout/payment/guest list is clean or scattered.
4. Prioritize events where WhatsApp or DMs are doing too much work.

Best pain signals:

- "DM for tickets"
- WhatsApp as checkout
- Manual confirmation
- Flyer-only promotion
- No trusted ticket page
- No clean guest list or repeat buyer loop

Best first ask:

```text
Keep WhatsApp for conversation. Use Mingla for the page, payment, ticket, guest list, and proof. Send one flyer and we will turn it into a cleaner checkout test.
```

Lagos weekly target:

- 30 new A/B leads
- 75 touches across Instagram/WhatsApp/email where appropriate
- 5 replies
- 3 asset asks
- 1 flyer-to-page pilot

## 8. Quality Gates

No lead moves to `prospect` unless it has:

- City
- Segment
- Source URL or field note
- Pain signal
- Lead score
- First ask
- Next action date

No first touch is sent unless it has:

- Personalization token
- One specific observed moment
- One small next step
- No guaranteed-demand claim

No lead moves to `onboarded` unless:

- The prospect said yes, sent an asset, or agreed to a test.
- A `Creator Onboarding` card exists.
- The Supply CRM card links to the onboarding card.

No lead moves to `active` unless:

- A real page/campaign/link is live and shared, or
- There is repeat publishing/share behavior, or
- There is buyer/lead/revenue/proof activity.

Decision rules:

- Change the message if sourcing quality is high but replies are weak.
- Change the source if lead quality is low.
- Park the city/segment if there are no upcoming moments, no owner access, and no useful objections after two focused weeks.

## 10. Objection SOP

When an objection appears, do not argue. Diagnose the fear and turn it into the next proof step.

| Objection | What it means | Response | Proof step |
|---|---|---|---|
| We already use Eventbrite/Posh/Tix Africa | Ticketing feels handled | "That makes sense if the only job is selling a ticket. Where does the process still break: promotion, checkout, door, or getting people back?" | Mirror one live listing |
| We promote on Instagram | Attention feels enough | "Instagram is great for awareness. The gap is what happens after someone says this looks good." | Turn one post/flyer into a page |
| We use WhatsApp | Conversation is the operating layer | "Keep WhatsApp for conversation. Use Mingla for the page, payment, guest list, ticket, and proof." | Share one Mingla link in WhatsApp |
| Will this bring people in? | Demand proof is missing | "I will not promise magic demand. The first test is cleaner discovery, sharing, checkout, and tracking." | Define a 7-day goal |
| We do not have time | Setup cost feels too high | "Send one flyer/menu/event. We will draft the page and you approve it." | Founder-assisted setup |
| We have a website | They think Mingla replaces presence | "Your website tells people who you are. Mingla turns a specific moment into action." | Compare one event path |

## 11. Done Definition

A prospecting week is done when:

- Every touched lead has a current ClickUp status.
- Every active lead has a next action date.
- Every yes has a Creator Onboarding card.
- Every day has an Activity Log card.
- Friday review names the city/segment/message verdict.
- At least one proof item, objection, or product learning is captured.
