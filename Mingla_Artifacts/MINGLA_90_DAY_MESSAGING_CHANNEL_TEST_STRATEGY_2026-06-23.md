# Mingla 90-Day Messaging + Channel Testing Strategy

Date: 2026-06-23
Owner: Seth / Mingla Growth
Planning window: next 3 months
Priority: Business supply first, Explorer demand second

## Main Strategy

For the next 90 days, Mingla should not try to scale every channel.

Mingla should run a focused testing system that proves:

1. Which business ICPs are most likely to activate.
2. Which messages make them reply, book, publish, and share.
3. Which channels produce activated business supply at the lowest effort and cost.
4. Which Explorer messages create demand around the supply we are building.
5. Which channels deserve scale after the app and tracking are ready.

Business users are the priority because they create both revenue potential and supply. Every activated business gives Mingla more places, events, offers, pages, ticket inventory, QR links, and local proof for Explorers.

The strategic bet:

If Mingla can repeatedly turn operator moments into published, shareable, revenue-capable pages, then Explorer acquisition becomes easier because the app has real local inventory, not just a promise.

## North Star

### 90-Day North Star Metric

Weekly Activated Supply.

Definition:

The number of business users each week who publish and share at least one Mingla page, event, offer, trip, experience, or venue moment that can generate a lead, RSVP, ticket, booking, purchase, or qualified Explorer action.

Why this is the right 90-day North Star:

- It prioritizes business users without ignoring Explorer demand.
- It measures actual marketplace supply, not just signups.
- It creates surfaces that can be shared across Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, QR codes, local blogs, search, and paid ads.
- It is closer to revenue than followers, impressions, or raw installs.

Business:

- Call/demo/setup rate.
- Business signup rate.

## 90-Day Funnel Targets

These are operating targets, not promises. Adjust weekly based on real baseline data.

### Business Supply Funnel

90-day target:

- 900 qualified business prospects sourced.
- 600 personalized business touches sent.
- 180 replies or meaningful engagements.
- 90 qualified conversations.
- 60 setup/demo calls or async onboarding flows started.
- 45 business accounts created.
- 30 published business pages/events/offers/experiences.
- 20 businesses share their Mingla link or QR.
- 10 businesses receive a first demand signal.
- 5 businesses show first revenue signal or buyer/lead evidence.
- 3 businesses repeat with a second page/event/campaign.

Primary conversion benchmark:

- Contact to reply: 20-30%.
- Reply to qualified conversation: 40-60%.
- Qualified conversation to published page: 30-50%.
- Published page to shared link/QR: 60%+.

If publish rate is below 30%, the onboarding ask is too heavy or the value proposition is unclear.

### Explorer Demand Funnel

90-day target:

- 5,000 tracked Explorer landing/social/profile clicks.
- 1,500 beta access or app-store-intent clicks.
- 600 TestFlight joins / installs / qualified beta signups.
- 300 activated Explorers.
- 150 first saves.
- 75 first shares.
- 40 first boards/shortlists.
- 25 first RSVP/ticket/booking/lead actions.

Primary conversion benchmark:

- Click to beta/install intent: 20-35%.
- Install/join to first meaningful action: 30-50%.
- First action to repeat weekly action: 20%+.

If clicks are strong but activation is weak, stop scaling ads and fix onboarding, supply relevance, or recommendation confidence.

## ICP Focus For The Next 90 Days

### Tier 1 Business ICPs

Focus most outbound, founder time, content, and proof on these.

1. Event-forward independent hospitality operators.
2. Nightlife and live-entertainment venues.
3. Recurring promoters and community organizers.
4. Creator-hosts and micro-community curators.

Why:

- They have urgent moments.
- They already promote through social, DMs, flyers, WhatsApp, and scattered links.
- They can publish fast.
- They create visible supply.
- They can produce revenue signals through tickets, leads, bookings, and repeat events.

### Tier 2 Business ICPs

Test selectively when the opportunity is warm.

5. Experience, activity, and trip operators.
6. Pop-up, market, and temporary experience operators.

Why:

- Strong fit, but often more operationally complex.
- Best tested through warm intros, creators, and specific upcoming moments.

### Hold / Opportunistic

7. Workshop-first wellness and lifestyle operators.
8. Multi-location hospitality groups.

Why:

- Good later, but not the cleanest first proof.
- Multi-location groups can slow the cycle with procurement and reporting needs.
- Wellness operators may need scheduling/membership workflows Mingla should not lead with yet.

### Explorer ICPs To Support Business Supply

Prioritize Explorer demand that can consume or share the supply we are onboarding.

1. Date-night planner.
2. Friend-group plan captain.
3. Bored local.
4. New-city social rebuilder.

Secondary:

5. Solo Explorer.
6. Occasion planner.

## Message Testing System

Every message test must answer:

- Who is the ICP?
- What pain is being named?
- What action do we want?
- What channel is being tested?
- What landing path or CTA receives the traffic?
- What success metric decides scale, iterate, or kill?

### Business Message Hypotheses

#### Message A: Get Found

Core line:

You built the vibe. Now people need to find it.

Best ICPs:

- Event-forward hospitality.
- Creator-hosts.
- Pop-ups.

Pain:

The business has something worth showing up for, but discovery is weak.

CTA:

Send us one upcoming moment and we will help turn it into a Mingla page people can discover and share.

Success signal:

Reply, page created, link shared.

#### Message B: Flyer To Campaign

Core line:

A flyer is not a campaign.

Best ICPs:

- Nightlife venues.
- Recurring promoters.
- Pop-ups.

Pain:

Attention is trapped in Instagram posts, DMs, screenshots, and scattered links.

CTA:

Let us turn one flyer into a live page with a clean action path.

Success signal:

Reply, event page published, ticket/RSVP/lead path live.

#### Message C: Stop DM Chaos

Core line:

Stop collecting payments, names, and guest lists in DMs.

Best ICPs:

- Nightlife venues.
- Recurring promoters.
- Experience/trip operators.
- Lagos operators using WhatsApp/DM workflows.

Pain:

Manual confirmation, payment, guest list, and follow-up cost time and trust.

CTA:

Move one event from DM chaos to a Mingla page with checkout, list, and share link.

Success signal:

Checkout/ticket/RSVP flow set up, guest list used, first buyer/lead.

#### Message D: Repeat Audience

Core line:

Every event should build the audience for the next one.

Best ICPs:

- Recurring promoters.
- Community organizers.
- Creator-hosts.

Pain:

Every event feels like starting from zero.

CTA:

Move one recurring series to Mingla and use it to build the next audience.

Success signal:

Recurring event published, repeat draft created, prior attendees/leads captured.

#### Message E: Turn Taste Into Movement

Core line:

Turn your taste into real-life movement.

Best ICPs:

- Creator-hosts.
- Micro-community curators.
- Local tastemakers.

Pain:

Attention does not become owned buyers, attendees, venue leverage, or repeatable real-life behavior.

CTA:

Create one Mingla-powered guide, outing, event, or venue collaboration.

Success signal:

Creator shares link, audience clicks/saves/RSVPs, business or venue collaboration starts.

### Explorer Message Hypotheses

#### Message 1: Date Night Rescue

Core line:

Date night should not require a research department.

Primary action:

Try Mingla and save one date idea.

#### Message 2: Group Chat CPR

Core line:

Pick the plan before the group chat dies.

Primary action:

Start or share a shortlist.

#### Message 3: Same Three Places

Core line:

You are not bored. You are going to the same places.

Primary action:

Find and save something new nearby.

#### Message 4: New City Rhythm

Core line:

Find your places. Build your rhythm.

Primary action:

Save three places or plans in your city.

## Channel Rules

### Rule 1: Business Supply Gets 70% Of Growth Effort

For the next 90 days:

- 70% business supply acquisition and activation.
- 20% Explorer demand tied to available supply.
- 10% brand/investor/PR credibility.

Do not let consumer content become a distraction from supply density.

### Rule 2: No Channel Scales Until It Produces Activation

Awareness is not enough.

Scale only when a channel produces:

- Activated business supply.
- Activated Explorers.
- Qualified replies.
- Published pages.
- Shared links.
- First demand or revenue signals.

### Rule 3: Every Business Outreach Test Must Use One Clear Ask

Bad ask:

Join our platform.

Good ask:

Send one flyer, menu item, offer, recurring night, or event and we will turn it into a clean Mingla page you can share.

### Rule 4: Every Business Page Needs A Distribution Plan

Publishing alone is not activation.

Every page must have at least three distribution paths:

- Business Instagram story/post.
- QR code or door/table placement.
- Founder/Mingla spotlight or local guide placement.
- Creator/community share.
- WhatsApp/Telegram/Facebook group where appropriate.
- Retargeting audience when available.

### Rule 5: Paid Ads Start As Tests, Not Growth Theater

Allowed in month 1:

- Retargeting.
- Search ads to high-intent landing pages.
- Small creative tests against proven organic hooks.

Not allowed in month 1:

- Broad install campaigns optimized only for cheap installs.
- Scaling paid before first meaningful activation tracking works.

### Rule 6: Local Community Channels Require Value First

Reddit, Facebook groups, WhatsApp, Telegram, and local forums should not receive copy-paste promo.

Use them for:

- Useful answers.
- City guides.
- Event/plan recommendations.
- Founder/community participation.
- Admin partnerships.

Only mention Mingla when it makes the answer more useful.

### Rule 7: One ICP, One Message, One Channel, One Decision Rule

Do not test a mush.

Every experiment must isolate:

- ICP.
- Message.
- Channel.
- CTA.
- Landing path.
- Success metric.
- Decision date.

## Channel Testing Plan

### Tier 1 Channels

Use these immediately.

#### Cold DM

Primary use:

Business supply acquisition.

Best ICPs:

- Event-forward hospitality.
- Nightlife venues.
- Recurring promoters.
- Creator-hosts.
- Pop-ups.

Test volume:

- 50-75 personalized DMs per week.

Success rule:

- Scale if reply rate is 20%+ and publish rate from replies is 20%+.
- Iterate if replies are positive but setup does not happen.
- Kill message if reply rate is below 10% after 75 sends.

#### Cold Calling

Primary use:

Fast qualification and operator learning.

Best ICPs:

- Triangle restaurants, bars, cafes, venues, and event-forward hospitality.

Test volume:

- 25-40 calls per week.

Success rule:

- Scale if 25%+ of reached decision-makers agree to send an asset, take a setup call, or review a draft page.

#### Cold Email

Primary use:

Structured follow-up and higher-consideration operators.

Best ICPs:

- Experience operators.
- Hospitality groups.
- Wellness/workshop operators.
- Venues with public event/programming contacts.

Test volume:

- 75-100 emails per week.

Success rule:

- Scale if reply rate is 8-12%+ and meeting/setup rate is 3-5%+.

#### Instagram Organic + DM

Primary use:

Business discovery, operator trust, creator sourcing, and Explorer demand.

Weekly rhythm:

- 3 Business posts.
- 3 Explorer posts.
- 2 founder/operator story posts.
- 25-50 targeted DMs.
- 5 collab post asks.

Success rule:

- Scale formats that produce qualified DMs, shares, saves, profile clicks, or business replies.

#### Creator / Influencer Marketing

Primary use:

Local trust and distribution.

Test volume:

- 20 creator conversations per month.
- 5 creator pilots in 90 days.

Success rule:

- Scale creators who produce tracked clicks, business intros, saves, RSVP/ticket interest, or activated Explorers.

### Tier 2 Channels

Use after landing pages and tracking are ready.

#### Search Ads

Primary use:

Capture high-intent business and Explorer demand.

Business tests:

- event marketing for restaurants
- sell tickets for event
- promote bar event
- guest list app
- event page for restaurant

Explorer tests:

- date night Raleigh
- things to do Raleigh this weekend
- group activities Raleigh
- solo things to do Durham

Success rule:

- Scale if landing page conversion beats organic baseline and cost per activated lead is acceptable.

#### Meta Ads

Primary use:

Retargeting and creative amplification.

Start with:

- Retargeting site visitors.
- Retargeting Instagram engagers.
- Business lead form tests.
- Boosting proven Reels, not untested creative.

Success rule:

- Scale if cost per qualified business lead or activated Explorer beats organic-assisted alternatives.

#### TikTok Ads

Primary use:

Scale proven Explorer hooks.

Start with:

- Spark Ads from posts that already got saves, shares, comments, or profile clicks.

Success rule:

- Scale only if downstream activation is visible.

#### Local Blogs / Newsletters

Primary use:

Credibility, backlinks, and city legitimacy.

Test volume:

- 10 pitches per month.

Success rule:

- Scale angles that produce backlinks, referral clicks, business inbound, or founder credibility.

### Tier 3 Channels

Use selectively.

#### Reddit

Use for:

- Research.
- Useful city answers.
- High-intent landing page tests.
- Paid tests after message-market fit.

Rule:

No lazy promotion.

#### Facebook Groups

Use for:

- Local community distribution.
- Event/operator discovery.
- Business groups.

Rule:

Admin-first or value-first.

#### WhatsApp / Telegram

Use for:

- Lagos.
- Promoters.
- Niche communities.
- Creator-hosted events.

Rule:

Partner with admins and hosts. Do not spam.

#### X / Threads

Use for:

- Founder POV.
- Investor/partner credibility.
- Category insight.

Rule:

Not a core acquisition channel for the next 90 days.

#### Snapchat

Use for:

- Nightlife, campus, and event-specific geo moments.

Rule:

Do not run broad Snapchat spend until a specific event/campus/nightlife wedge exists.

## 90-Day Roadmap

### Month 1: Prove The Message And The Ask

Primary question:

Which business ICPs will reply and publish when we make one clear ask?

Actions:

- Source 300 qualified business prospects.
- Send 200 personalized touches.
- Run 100 cold DMs, 60 emails, 40 calls.
- Test 5 business message families.
- Build 10 draft/example Mingla pages for high-fit prospects.
- Publish 8-10 real business pages/events/offers.
- Launch or update business landing pages for top 4 ICPs.
- Publish 12 Business posts, 12 Explorer posts, and 8 founder/proof posts.
- Start creator list with 40 local creators and outreach to 20.

Month 1 SMART goals:

- By the end of day 30, source 300 qualified business prospects across Tier 1 ICPs.
- By the end of day 30, get 50 business replies or meaningful engagements.
- By the end of day 30, publish 8 business pages/events/offers.
- By the end of day 30, identify the top 2 business messages by reply-to-publish rate.

Decision:

- Kill any message with less than 10% reply rate after 75 touches.
- Scale any message with 20%+ reply rate and at least 20% reply-to-publish conversion.

### Month 2: Prove Channel Fit And Distribution

Primary question:

Which channels produce activated business supply and Explorer demand around that supply?

Actions:

- Source another 300 business prospects.
- Send 200 more personalized touches.
- Double down on top 2 business messages.
- Test cold DM vs calls vs email by ICP.
- Run 3 creator/business collaborations.
- Run 3 business page distribution pilots with QR + social + creator/local guide.
- Launch 2-3 small retargeting tests.
- Launch search tests for business high-intent pages.
- Publish 12 Business posts, 12 Explorer posts, and 8 founder/proof posts.

Month 2 SMART goals:

- By the end of day 60, reach 20 total published business pages/events/offers.
- By the end of day 60, get 12 businesses to share their Mingla link or QR.
- By the end of day 60, produce 5 first demand signals from business pages.
- By the end of day 60, identify the top 2 channels by cost/effort per activated business.

Decision:

- Scale the top 2 channels.
- Pause any channel that produces attention but no publish/share/demand behavior.

### Month 3: Scale Winners And Create Repeatability

Primary question:

Can the best message-channel combinations repeat weekly?

Actions:

- Source another 300 business prospects.
- Send 200 more personalized touches.
- Turn top 2 messages into templates, landing pages, posts, scripts, and ads.
- Run 5 creator/local proof pilots.
- Run retargeting against business and Explorer audiences.
- Run search ads only on pages that already convert.
- Produce 5 public business success stories.
- Build a repeatable weekly dashboard.
- Start a referral/intro ask for businesses and creators.

Month 3 SMART goals:

- By the end of day 90, reach 30 total published business pages/events/offers.
- By the end of day 90, get 20 businesses to share their Mingla link or QR.
- By the end of day 90, get 10 businesses to receive first demand signal.
- By the end of day 90, get 5 businesses to show first revenue/buyer/lead proof.
- By the end of day 90, get 3 businesses to publish a second page/event/campaign.
- By the end of day 90, know the top 3 repeatable acquisition motions for the next quarter.

Decision:

- Turn winners into the Q2 operating system.
- Cut channels that cannot produce activated supply, activated demand, or proof.

## SMART Goals

### Goal 1: Business Supply Activation

Specific:

Activate 30 business users by getting them to publish and share a Mingla page, event, offer, venue moment, trip, or experience.

Measurable:

30 published pages/events/offers, 20 shared links/QRs, 10 first demand signals, 5 first revenue signals.

Achievable:

Requires 600 personalized touches over 90 days and a 5% touch-to-publish rate.

Relevant:

Business supply creates revenue potential and gives Explorers real inventory.

Time-bound:

By day 90.

### Goal 2: Message-Market Fit

Specific:

Identify the top 2 business messages by reply-to-publish rate.

Measurable:

Each winning message must have at least 75 touches, 20%+ reply rate, and 20%+ reply-to-publish conversion.

Achievable:

Test 5 message families across 600 touches.

Relevant:

The winning message becomes the base for landing pages, ads, outreach, social, and sales scripts.

Time-bound:

By day 45 for first read; by day 90 for final decision.

### Goal 3: Channel Fit

Specific:

Identify the top 3 repeatable acquisition channels for activated business supply.

Measurable:

Each winning channel must produce at least 5 published businesses or a clear path to 5 published businesses per month.

Achievable:

Test cold DM, cold calling, cold email, Instagram DM, creator partnerships, search, Meta retargeting, and local blogs.

Relevant:

Mingla needs repeatable supply acquisition before broad Explorer scaling.

Time-bound:

By day 90.

### Goal 4: Explorer Demand Around Supply

Specific:

Generate activated Explorer demand around the supply being onboarded.

Measurable:

600 beta joins/installs/qualified signups, 300 activated Explorers, 150 saves, 75 shares, 25 RSVP/ticket/booking/lead actions.

Achievable:

Use Instagram, TikTok, creator posts, city landing pages, local guides, and retargeting.

Relevant:

Explorer demand gives businesses proof that Mingla can create attention and action.

Time-bound:

By day 90.

### Goal 5: Proof System

Specific:

Produce proof assets that make the next sales and growth cycle easier.

Measurable:

5 business success stories, 10 quote/proof snippets, 5 before/after page examples, 3 creator/local proof examples.

Achievable:

Capture proof from the 30 activated businesses and 5 creator pilots.

Relevant:

Proof lowers trust friction across cold outreach, landing pages, ads, investor updates, and sales calls.

Time-bound:

By day 90.

## What Not To Do

Do not:

- Run broad paid app install campaigns before activation tracking is reliable.
- Create content for every ICP at once.
- Treat geography as the ICP.
- Build Explorer demand in cities where Mingla cannot show useful supply.
- Spend founder time on channels that cannot produce replies, published pages, or proof.
- Let social media become the work instead of a distribution layer for the work.
