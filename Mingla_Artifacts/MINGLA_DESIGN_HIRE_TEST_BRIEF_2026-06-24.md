# Mingla Creative Test Case — Event-Organizer Launch Campaign

## Why this test exists

We're hiring people who can make Mingla *feel* like Mingla — warm, alive, a little playful, and impossible to scroll past. This isn't a trivia quiz or a take-home that gets thrown away. It's a real campaign brief for a real audience: **event organizers**, the people who throw the parties, pop-ups, dinners, mixers, meetups, workshops, and concerts that make a city worth living in.

We want to see how you think, how you craft, and how you'd actually launch something. So we're asking you to do the thing the job is: **live in our product, understand the organizer, and produce a tight three-asset campaign that would genuinely make an organizer stop and sign up.** Everything you build here is judged on taste, judgment, and execution — never on whether you personally own the software to do it (more on that below). Make something you'd be proud to put in your portfolio.

---

## What we're really evaluating

This brief tests **two distinct competencies.** You may be **one person who does both**, or **a pair splitting the work** — either is welcome. We will score each competency on its own merits, so make it clear who owned what if you're a team.

**1. Graphics & Motion Designer — the craft.**
You own visual execution: the motion graphic, the video edit, the still graphic. We're looking at design-system fluency (can you hold a brand together across three pieces?), motion storytelling and easing, edit rhythm and beat-matching, typographic taste, color discipline, and pixel-level polish. Does it *feel* designed, not decorated?

**2. Brand & Community Manager — the thinking and the voice.**
You own the campaign brain: the concept, the positioning angle for organizers, the copy and captions and CTAs, the channel plan across Instagram / TikTok / Threads / X, and the logic that makes three assets add up to one coherent campaign. We're looking at strategy, narrative, audience insight, and whether the words sound like Mingla — warm, confident, human, a little playful, never corporate, never a "dating-app."

A strong submission nails both. If you're stronger on one side, show us your strongest side at full strength and be honest about the other — we'd rather see real range than a flat average.

## Step 1 — Live the product (do this before you design anything)

You cannot write a campaign for a product you've only read about. So first, **go be an organizer.**

1. **Sign in** to **business.usemingla.com** (the Mingla Business / organizer side of the platform). If you need a login, a test brand, or you hit anything blocked — **ask us** (see "Tools & access" below). Don't fake your way around it.
2. **Set up your brand / public page.** Walk through the brand setup so you see how an organizer's public branded presence comes together — name, logo, colors, the page a guest actually lands on.
3. **Create a FREE RSVP event, end to end.** Go all the way through the real creation flow so you feel every screen an organizer touches:
   - Event title
   - Cover media (image or video)
   - Date & time
   - Venue / location
   - **RSVP (free) vs. paid ticketing** — choose RSVP for this walkthrough, but notice where the paid path lives
   - Capacity / guest limit
4. **Find your shareable public event page.** This is the hero of the whole product promise — *one beautiful link you share everywhere.* Look at it the way a guest would.
5. **Open the guest / RSVP list** so you understand how an organizer sees who's coming and owns that guest relationship.
6. **Open the marketing / blast surface** so you see how an organizer messages their guest list (email/SMS).

**Screenshot requirement.** As you go, capture the **real screens** — the creation flow, the public event page, the RSVP list, the blast surface. We want your assets to use **actual product UI**, not invented mockups. **Accuracy beats invention every time.** If a screen looks off or you're unsure it's the latest, ask — don't guess.

Treat this step as research, not busywork. The best submissions will quote a real moment from this flow ("spin up a page in under five minutes") because they *felt* it.

## Step 2 — Write the campaign brief

Before (or alongside) the assets, write a **short campaign brief** — one to two pages, delivered as the `CAMPAIGN_BRIEF` doc in the shared folder. This is where the Brand & Community Manager competency lives. Cover:

- **Concept / big idea.** The single creative thread that ties all three assets together. One sentence you could say out loud.
- **Organizer insight.** What's the real tension in an event organizer's life that Mingla resolves? (Scattered DMs? Ugly RSVP forms? No-shows? Chasing payments? Not owning their guest list?) Show us you *get* this person.
- **Positioning angle.** How you'd frame Mingla to organizers specifically — the promise is speed + polish + owning your guest relationship: spin up a free RSVP event in minutes, get a beautiful branded page, share one link, collect RSVPs and guest info, message your list, and sell paid tickets with all-in upfront pricing and fast payouts when you're ready. Pick the sharpest angle; don't list all of them.
- **Channel plan.** How the three assets deploy across **Instagram (Reels/Stories/feed), TikTok, Threads, and X** — what leads, what supports, what the posting logic is, and how the captions/CTA shift per channel.
- **Voice.** A few lines showing you can write *as Mingla* — warm, confident, human, a little playful. Your captions and CTAs go here too.

Keep it tight and decision-grade. We'd rather read one page of sharp thinking than five pages of filler.

## Step 3 — The three assets

Three deliverables. Each has a **north-star benchmark** on Instagram — study it closely. You're matching the *qualities and bar*, not copying it. Use real Mingla brand assets and real product UI throughout.

### Asset 1 — Motion graphic

An animated graphic / kinetic-typography / branded motion piece — the kind of thing that loops in a feed and lands one idea perfectly.

- **Benchmark (north star):** https://www.instagram.com/p/DSVXztjAHuu/
- **Qualities to match:** tight kinetic typography, satisfying easing (nothing linear or stiff), confident on-brand color, **one clear message** (not five), loop-friendly so it reads on repeat, and sound-design-aware (built to work with audio, even if final audio is added later).
- **Format & export:**
  - **1080×1920 vertical** (Reels/Stories) **+ a 1080×1080 square cut**
  - **5–12 seconds**
  - Export **MP4 (H.264)** **plus the source project file** (After Effects, etc.)
- **Folder:** `candidate-deliverables/01-motion-graphic`

### Asset 2 — Video edit (collage of AI-generated videos)

A reel assembled from **multiple AI-generated video clips** (Sora / Runway / Kling / Pika / Veo — your pick, and ask us for credits) cut to a beat, telling the story: *throw an unforgettable event, powered by Mingla.*

- **Benchmark (north star):** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DB1ZHVavU3t/
- **Qualities to match:** a **cohesive grade/look across disparate AI clips** (they should feel like one film, not a clip dump), **beat-matched cuts**, real momentum, a **narrative arc** (tension → Mingla → the unforgettable event), captions/text overlays that carry the message, and a clean **Mingla CTA** to close.
- **Format & export:**
  - **1080×1920**
  - **12–30 seconds**
  - Export **MP4**
  - Deliver the **clip list + the exact prompts you used + the project file** (we want to see your process)
- **Folder:** `candidate-deliverables/02-video-collage`

### Asset 3 — Still graphic

A single hero poster / key visual for the event-organizer campaign — the one image that stops the feed.

- **Benchmark (north star):** https://www.instagram.com/p/DTl0d2tAAcM/
- **Qualities to match:** **strong focal hierarchy** (eye knows where to go), confident type, brand-color discipline, **one clear message + one CTA**, feed-stopping at thumbnail size.
- **Format & export:**
  - **1080×1350 (4:5 feed)** **+ 1080×1920 (story)**
  - Export **PNG** **plus the layered source** (Figma or PSD)
- **Folder:** `candidate-deliverables/03-still-graphic`

> You can't open the benchmark links inside this brief, and that's intentional — go study each one directly on Instagram before you start. Read the *craft*: the pacing, the type, the restraint, the way one idea is delivered. Then make something that hits that bar in **Mingla's** voice and brand. We're looking for transferable quality, not a remake.

## How we'll score it

Weighted rubric. Both competencies are represented; a team submission is scored on each person's owned work.

| Criterion | Weight | What "great" looks like |
|-----------|--------|--------------------------|
| **Brand fidelity** | 15% | Exact colors, correct fonts, correct logo usage, on-voice — feels unmistakably Mingla across all three assets. |
| **Craft & polish** | 15% | Pixel-level care; type, spacing, grade, and finish are clean and intentional. |
| **Motion & edit quality** | 15% | Easing, rhythm, beat-matching, cohesive grade across AI clips, satisfying loops, clean CTA landings. |
| **Strategic thinking** | 15% | Sharp organizer insight, a real big idea, a channel plan that makes sense, three assets that cohere into one campaign. |
| **Copy & voice** | 15% | Captions and CTAs that sound like Mingla and would actually move an organizer to act. |
| **Product accuracy** | 15% | Real product UI and real screens used; the campaign reflects how Mingla *actually* works for organizers (proof you did Step 1). |
| **Ability to ask for what's needed** | 10% | Smart, specific requests for tools/access/clarity at the right moments — shows you know what great execution requires. |

We weight **thinking and craft equally** on purpose. A beautiful asset attached to a hollow idea scores the same as a brilliant idea executed flatly — we want both.
