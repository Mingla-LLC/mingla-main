# Mingla Vetted ICP Segments

ClickUp target: `Mingla Growth` -> `01 Sprint Board` -> `Strategy & Docs` -> `Strategy Doc`
Purpose: product, GTM, sales, content, prospecting, and sprint prioritization reference

## Summary

Seth's correction is right: geography is not an ICP. Lagos, DC, and the Triangle are market wedges or launch lanes. They should influence sourcing, channels, payments, messaging, and proof, but they should not define the ICP.

The corrected Mingla ICP set has 14 operating ICPs:

- 8 business / supply ICPs.
- 6 consumer / Explorer ICPs.

The old `Lagos / Nigeria venues and brands` item has been retired as an ICP and moved into the geography / market overlay section. `Owner-operated story-rich` is also not a standalone ICP; it is a high-value qualifier that can apply across restaurants, venues, creators, pop-ups, and experience brands.

## ICP Test

A real ICP must answer all five questions:

1. Who is the buyer or user?
2. What job are they trying to get done?
3. What painful workaround do they use today?
4. What trigger makes the pain urgent now?
5. What Mingla activation proves they are real?

If the label only says where they are, how they look, or what vibe they have, it is not an ICP. It is a market, qualifier, source list, or messaging overlay.

## Geography Is A Market Overlay

Use geography this way:

| Geography | What it changes | What it does not change |
|---|---|---|
| Triangle | Source lists, field visits, founder-led access, local proof density | The underlying ICP |
| DC | Associations, restaurant/event calendars, partnership paths, competitive density | The underlying ICP |
| Lagos | WhatsApp/DM workflows, trust, payment expectations, event-platform norms, payout context | The underlying ICP |

Example:

- Bad ICP label: `Lagos venues`.
- Better ICP label: `Nightlife and live-entertainment venues`.
- Lagos overlay: `Prioritize venues/promoters where WhatsApp/DMs handle payment, confirmation, and guest list today.`

## Qualifiers, Not ICPs

These are useful filters, but they should not be top-level ICPs:

| Qualifier | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Owner-operated | Faster decision cycle, more founder empathy, less procurement |
| Story-rich / vibe-rich | Easier to package into consumer-facing discovery |
| Weak public link | Clear before/after value for Mingla pages |
| Active Instagram | Easier sourcing and first-touch personalization |
| Upcoming moment | Makes the pain urgent |
| DM / WhatsApp / spreadsheet workflow | Strong pain signal for checkout, guest list, and follow-up |
| Recurring programming | Creates repeat buyer and retention potential |
| Warm intro | Increases trust and conversion |

## Business / Supply ICPs

These ICPs belong primarily to Mingla Business, public pages, seller onboarding, ticketing/booking/checkout, event operations, creator onboarding, and supply-side sales.

### 1. Event-Forward Independent Hospitality Operators

Buyer:

- Owner, general manager, marketer, events manager, chef-owner, or founder-operator.

Definition:

- Independent restaurants, bars, cafes, lounges, breweries, or hospitality brands that use specific moments to drive demand: brunch, live music, tastings, private dinners, menu drops, happy hours, community nights, limited offers, or seasonal campaigns.

Job:

- Turn a specific venue moment into booked demand, visits, tickets, leads, or repeat customers.

Current workaround:

- Instagram posts, stories, flyers, link-in-bio tools, Google Business Profile, website pages, OpenTable/Resy, Square/Toast, DMs, staff memory.

Pain:

- The place may have real vibe, but the moment is not packaged into a clean action path.
- Attention does not reliably become bookings, buyers, or repeat audience.

Trigger:

- New menu, slow night, brunch push, weekly programming, anniversary, private event, live music, local press, seasonal offer.

Mingla wedge:

- Turn one venue moment into a clean page, link, QR, and shareable plan people can discover, book, buy from, or send to friends.

Best first ask:

- Send one menu item, flyer, offer, or recurring night and let Mingla turn it into a page/share-link test.

Activation signal:

- Public page published, QR/link shared, first booking/lead/buyer, or repeat weekly page.

Disqualify when:

- No owner/operator access, no upcoming moment, no willingness to share a link, or no pain with the current workflow.

### 2. Nightlife And Live-Entertainment Venues

Buyer:

- Owner, promoter, GM, events lead, talent buyer, door manager, or nightlife marketer.

Definition:

- Bars, clubs, lounges, live-music rooms, performance venues, and entertainment spaces where event-night operations matter.

Job:

- Fill the room, sell tickets, manage guest list and comps, run the door, and keep people coming back.

Current workaround:

- Instagram, Eventbrite/Posh, spreadsheets, DMs, screenshots, WhatsApp, paper guest list, staff memory.

Pain:

- Promo, tickets, guest list, comps, scanning, and door decisions live across too many tools and people.
- Event-night chaos makes the operator lose time, trust, and repeat-buyer data.

Trigger:

- Upcoming party, DJ booking, live show, recurring weekly night, slow night, new event series, holiday weekend.

Mingla wedge:

- Run the night from listing to checkout to guest list to scanner to follow-up.

Best first ask:

- Give Mingla one upcoming event and one flyer to convert into a live page and door-ready test.

Activation signal:

- Ticket tier created, checkout live, scanner used, guest list exported, buyer list captured, or repeat event published.

Disqualify when:

- They only want guaranteed demand before testing or they have no operational pain with their current tool.

### 3. Recurring Promoters And Community Organizers

Buyer:

- Promoter, organizer, curator, community builder, creator-host, or venue programming lead.

Definition:

- People who run weekly, monthly, or repeated real-life events and need the audience to compound over time.

Job:

- Keep a recurring series growing without restarting from zero every time.

Current workaround:

- Instagram flyers, DMs, Eventbrite/Posh, WhatsApp, Google Forms, spreadsheets, manual reminders.

Pain:

- Every event feels like a fresh launch.
- Buyer lists, attendees, DMs, and past interest are scattered across tools.

Trigger:

- Weekly series, monthly party, recurring brunch, open mic, trivia night, creator meetup, community event.

Mingla wedge:

- One event should create the audience and follow-up path for the next one.

Best first ask:

- Migrate one recurring event and use it to capture the repeat-audience loop.

Activation signal:

- First recurring event published, prior buyers contacted, next event drafted from the last one.

Disqualify when:

- They do not own the audience, cannot approve a page, or only want vague exposure.

### 4. Experience, Activity, And Trip Operators

Buyer:

- Founder, experience operator, trip host, workshop host, local activity brand, travel/community organizer.

Definition:

- Operators selling structured real-life experiences such as trips, crawls, tours, workshops, retreats, food/drink experiences, and multi-stop plans.

Job:

- Package and sell an experience without duct-taping discovery, checkout, intake, and operations.

Current workaround:

- Google Forms, Stripe links, DMs, WhatsApp, Eventbrite, spreadsheets, websites, manual email.

Pain:

- Itinerary, payment, intake, refund rules, promotion, and buyer communication are split across too many places.

Trigger:

- Flagship trip, ticketed workshop, local tour, food crawl, multi-stop night, retreat, seasonal experience.

Mingla wedge:

- Turn the experience into a page people can understand, buy, and share.

Best first ask:

- Build one flagship experience or trip page.

Activation signal:

- Experience page live, intake/payment flow tested, first buyer/lead captured, share link used.

Disqualify when:

- The use case needs complex tourism logistics or deep class booking before Mingla is ready.

### 5. Creator-Hosts And Micro-Community Curators

Buyer:

- Creator, curator, micro-influencer, community host, local tastemaker, niche event host.

Definition:

- People with audience trust or taste authority who can turn attention into a real-life event, guide, collaboration, or venue partnership.

Job:

- Turn taste, audience, and community into real-life movement and owned buyer/attendee relationships.

Current workaround:

- Instagram/TikTok posts, DMs, link-in-bio, affiliate links, ad hoc venue texts, spreadsheets.

Pain:

- Attention does not become owned buyers, tickets, venue leverage, or repeatable offline behavior.

Trigger:

- Audience asks for meetups, creator wants a venue collaboration, local guide content performs, brand partnership idea, niche community forms.

Mingla wedge:

- Turn taste into a creator-led event, guide, experience, or venue collaboration that can be shared, sold, and repeated.

Best first ask:

- Co-create one small-capacity creator-led event or guide.

Activation signal:

- Creator concept drafted, venue/experience page live, link shared, RSVP/tickets/leads captured.

Disqualify when:

- Audience is passive only, creator will not share, or the concept has no real-world action.

### 6. Pop-Up, Market, And Temporary Experience Operators

Buyer:

- Pop-up founder, vendor-market organizer, chef pop-up host, art/culture market organizer, workshop host.

Definition:

- Operators whose demand window is temporary and who need a clean page, checkout/RSVP, and share path before the moment disappears.

Job:

- Fill a temporary event, drop, market, workshop, or seasonal experience quickly.

Current workaround:

- Instagram flyers, Google Forms, DMs, Stripe links, Eventbrite, spreadsheets, manual payment confirmation.

Pain:

- Promotion is short-lived, interest is scattered, and manual confirmation creates avoidable friction.

Trigger:

- Vendor market, chef pop-up, limited drop, workshop, seasonal event, collaboration night.

Mingla wedge:

- Package the temporary moment into a clean page people can understand, book, buy, and share.

Best first ask:

- Publish one pop-up, workshop, or seasonal event page.

Activation signal:

- Page live, link shared, first booking/lead/buyer captured.

Disqualify when:

- The operator has no approval path, no asset, no date, or no plan to share the page.

### 7. Workshop-First Wellness And Lifestyle Operators

Buyer:

- Studio owner, instructor, facilitator, wellness host, lifestyle brand operator.

Definition:

- Wellness, fitness, lifestyle, and learning operators selling discrete workshops or special events, not deep recurring class schedules.

Job:

- Fill special sessions, workshops, retreats, community events, and one-off experiences.

Current workaround:

- Mindbody/ClassPass, Instagram, Google Forms, Stripe links, Eventbrite, DMs, spreadsheets.

Pain:

- Their core booking system may handle classes, but special events and community moments are still hard to package and promote.

Trigger:

- Special class, guest instructor, retreat, workshop, community event, seasonal wellness moment.

Mingla wedge:

- Create a clean event/experience page that sits beside the class system and is easier to share, buy, or RSVP to.

Best first ask:

- Publish one special workshop or community event page.

Activation signal:

- Event page live, link shared, first booking/lead captured.

Disqualify when:

- They need full class scheduling, memberships, or studio management as the first ask.

### 8. Multi-Location Hospitality Groups

Buyer:

- Owner, regional operator, marketing lead, finance lead, operations lead.

Definition:

- Hospitality groups with multiple venues or locations that need repeatable campaigns, permissions, reporting, and location-level performance visibility.

Job:

- Scale local moments and campaigns across locations without losing visibility or control.

Current workaround:

- Corporate website, separate location social pages, POS exports, Mailchimp, spreadsheets, agency reports.

Pain:

- Location-level activity is hard to package, compare, and manage cleanly.

Trigger:

- Warm intro, new location, multi-location event series, campaign calendar, need for finance/performance visibility.

Mingla wedge:

- Pilot one location or one campaign, then prove whether the playbook can repeat.

Best first ask:

- Run one location or one campaign across two locations.

Activation signal:

- One location goes live, campaign template repeats, finance/performance report matters to the buyer.

Disqualify when:

- Procurement is heavy, integration demands are high, or there is no urgent local campaign.

## Consumer / Explorer ICPs

These ICPs belong primarily to Mingla Explorer, consumer discovery, saved places/plans, voting/sharing, scheduling, RSVP, and consumer demand generation.

### 1. Date-Night Planner

User:

- Person responsible for finding a thoughtful plan for a partner, date, or early relationship moment.

Job:

- Find a plan that fits the person, mood, budget, timing, location, and level of effort.

Pain:

- Fear of being boring, picking the same three places, or over-researching.

Current workaround:

- Google Maps, TikTok, Instagram saves, Yelp, friend recs, old notes, endless tabs.

Explorer wedge:

- Date-night ideas that fit the two of you.

Activation signal:

- Saves a plan, shares it, schedules it, books/buys, or returns for another date-night search.

### 2. Friend-Group Plan Captain

User:

- The person trying to turn a vague group chat into an actual plan.

Job:

- Create a shortlist, get people to react/vote, and lock the plan before momentum dies.

Pain:

- Everyone says they are down, but nobody decides.

Current workaround:

- Group texts, Instagram links, screenshots, polls, calendar juggling, one person doing all the work.

Explorer wedge:

- Pick the plan before the chat dies.

Activation signal:

- Shares a shortlist, invites friends, gets votes/reactions, schedules, or RSVPs.

### 3. New-City Social Rebuilder

User:

- Someone who moved recently or is rebuilding local confidence and routines.

Job:

- Make the city feel livable, not just searchable.

Pain:

- They can find places, but do not know which ones fit their identity, rhythm, or social goals.

Current workaround:

- TikTok lists, Reddit, Google Maps, coworkers, Instagram, event newsletters, trial and error.

Explorer wedge:

- Find your places. Build your rhythm.

Activation signal:

- Saves multiple places, follows a vibe/category, schedules a solo or social outing, returns weekly.

### 4. Solo Explorer

User:

- Someone who wants to leave the house without needing a group.

Job:

- Find low-pressure solo-friendly places, resets, activities, and moments.

Pain:

- They want to go out but do not want a plan that feels awkward alone.

Current workaround:

- Coffee shops, bookstores, parks, bars with seating, wellness activities, saved Instagram posts, Google Maps.

Explorer wedge:

- Get out without guessing where to go.

Activation signal:

- Saves or schedules a solo-friendly place, visits, repeats a routine, or shares later with friends.

### 5. Bored Local

User:

- A local who feels bored but is actually stuck in a same-places loop.

Job:

- Make the city feel new again without starting a massive research project.

Pain:

- There are options nearby, but discovery feels stale and decision fatigue wins.

Current workaround:

- Same favorite places, Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps, event newsletters, friend suggestions.

Explorer wedge:

- You are not bored. You are going to the same places.

Activation signal:

- Discovers a new place, saves a fresh plan, shares it, or books/buys something outside the normal routine.

### 6. Occasion Planner

User:

- Person planning a birthday, anniversary, reunion, visitor weekend, celebration, or special night.

Job:

- Make the plan feel special without rebuilding the internet from scratch.

Pain:

- Higher stakes, more tabs, more people, more ways to disappoint.

Current workaround:

- Google searches, Instagram, Yelp, spreadsheets, group chat, restaurant sites, ticket/event platforms.

Explorer wedge:

- Make the plan feel special without the research spiral.

Activation signal:

- Builds a shortlist, shares it, books/buys, schedules, or coordinates with a group.

## Retired Or Reclassified Labels

| Old label | New classification | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lagos / Nigeria venues and brands | Geography / market overlay | Location changes sourcing, payment, trust, and channels; it does not define the buyer/job. |
| Owner-operated story-rich venues | Qualifier | Useful signal across several ICPs, but not a standalone buyer/job. |
| Independent restaurants, bars, and cafes | Tightened into event-forward hospitality | Too broad unless tied to a moment Mingla can package. |
| Wellness studios and pop-ups | Split into workshop-first wellness and temporary experience operators | Different buyer/workflow patterns. |
