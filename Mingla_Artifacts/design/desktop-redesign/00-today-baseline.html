Problem 1
Stranded column

A 440px column with 480px of empty canvas on each side. Looks like the app never finished loading.

Problem 2
Wasted real estate

On a 1440px display, >65% of pixels are unused. A desktop user can't see more events at once, can't compare orders side-by-side, can't open a detail without losing the list.

Problem 3
Mobile-only interaction model

Bottom-tabs only make sense on a thumb-reachable phone. Bottom sheets travel 800px to appear. No ⌘K, no keyboard nav, no multi-select, no drag-and-drop.
