About this experience
A guided three-stop crawl through DC's best small jazz rooms — a hidden basement listening bar, a corner speakeasy with a live trio, and a late-night rooftop set under the city lights. Your host knows the bartenders, skips the lines, and times each stop to the music. Come for the first impression, stay for the last set.
