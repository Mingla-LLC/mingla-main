About this trip
Six unhurried days on the cliffs of the Amalfi Coast — lemon groves, hidden swimming coves, a private boat day to Capri, and dinners that go long past sunset. Small group, real pace, no rushing between photo stops. You wake up where the locals do, eat where they eat, and leave knowing the coast the way a friend would show you.

Day by day
1
Day 1Sun · Sep 14
Arrive & settle in Positano
Private transfer from Naples, welcome aperitivo on the terrace, an easy walk to the beach as the light drops. The first evening is unstructured on purpose — get your bearings, find your café, watch the harbor lights come up.
2
Day 2Mon · Sep 15
Path of the Gods hike
A guided cliffside walk with views straight out to Capri, ending at a family farmhouse for a long lunch under the lemon trees.
3
Day 3Tue · Sep 16
Private boat to Capri
Charter the day around the Blue Grotto and the quiet swimming coves the day-trippers never reach, with a long seafood lunch served on board.
