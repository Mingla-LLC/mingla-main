Split Reserve CTA — one control, two options

Three sleek takes on a unified Reserve control to replace the two detached buttons. Each frame shows the docked (full-width, base of page) and floating (compact, scrolls with you) forms, plus the single-button fallback for trips with no payment plan. Both halves are real actions — each proceeds to cart with that pay choice; neither is a passive toggle.

Why B wins: it most unmistakably reads as ONE control (a single rounded shell folded by a seam), it uses the brand accent decisively but only once so the recommended "Pay in full" action leads without both halves screaming orange, and both halves stay honest actions. A is the safest if you want zero visual hierarchy between the two options; C is the fallback when amounts/translations get long and side-by-side would crowd. All three collapse cleanly to the single "Reserve my spot →" button when a trip has no plan.
