# Mingla ClickUp Growth System Audit

Date: 2026-06-24
Space: Mingla Growth
Purpose: Vet the 6-folder ClickUp operating structure and define how every folder, list, card type, and weekly ritual should work together.

## Executive Verdict

The six-folder architecture is good.

The current weakness is execution wiring.

The Sprint Board contains the 90-day plan, goals, and sprint cards. The other five folders mostly contain sample tasks. That means ClickUp has the strategy shell, but not yet the operating machine.

The fix is not another folder. The fix is a strict handoff system:

Backlog goal -> sprint card -> execution cards -> daily activity -> proof -> product/request/spend/standup feedback -> Friday redirect.

## Confirmed Folder Structure

| Folder | Lists | Current role | Health |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Sprint Board | Backlog, Sprint 1-7 | Strategy, goals, weekly sprint commitment | Strong plan shell, needs execution-card links |
| 02 Supply Pipeline | Supply CRM | Business lead relationship pipeline | Good status flow, real lead cards missing |
| 03 Demand & Outreach | Experiments, Influencer CRM, Activity Log | Message/channel testing, creators, daily activity | Good design, under-populated |
| 04 Content Engine | Creator Onboarding, Content Production | Fulfillment and content asset production | Good design, language too "page"-heavy |
| 05 Ops | Product Growth Requests, Spend Ledger | Growth-to-product bridge and budget control | Good support structure, needs evidence rules enforced |
| 06 Feedback & Re-direct | Standups | Daily blockers and weekly redirect inputs | Useful but too isolated unless tied to Friday review |

## Operating Model

ClickUp should run as one system:

1. Backlog defines the 90-day goals.
2. Sprint lists define what must happen during each two-week window.
3. Supply CRM tracks every business/operator relationship.
4. Experiments tracks every message/channel hypothesis.
5. Activity Log tracks daily touches and distribution actions.
6. Creator Onboarding tracks post-yes activation fulfillment.
7. Content Production creates the assets required by experiments, onboarding, and proof.
8. Influencer CRM tracks creators as partners, not content ideas.
9. Spend Ledger tracks every paid dollar connected to an experiment or creator.
10. Product Growth Requests turns repeated friction into product work.
11. Standups expose blockers and feed Friday redirect.

Weekly Activated Supply.

Definition:

The number of business users each week who create, publish, and share at least one real Mingla activation that can generate a lead, RSVP, ticket, booking, purchase, buyer contact, qualified Explorer action, partner proof, or repeat-publish signal.

## Folder 01: Sprint Board

### Role

The Sprint Board is the command layer. It should not hold every operational detail. It should hold goals, sprint commitments, and review cards.

### Current Strengths

- Backlog has clear 90-day North Star.
- Goal cards exist.
- Sprint 1-7 lists exist.
- Sprint cards are sequenced across supply, message testing, channels, Explorer demand, and proof.

### Current Problems

- Some cards still use "pages" as the main unit.
- Sprint cards are not linked to execution cards.
- Goal cards have no subtasks or linked execution cards.
- The execution folders still contain mostly sample tasks.
- Sprint card acceptance criteria do not always specify which list receives the working card.

### Required Rule

Every Sprint Board card must name:

- Goal code.
- ICP.
- City / market.
- Activation motion.
- Primary channel.
- Message being tested.
- CTA.
- Execution list(s).
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Metric.
- Friday decision rule.

### Sprint Card Completion Definition

A sprint card is complete only when:

- The execution cards were created in the right lists.
- Daily activity was logged.
- The metric was captured.
- The Friday verdict was written.
- Any proof, blocker, spend, or product friction was routed to the right folder.

### Required Sprint Board Fixes

Rename page-heavy sprint cards:

| Current wording | Replace with |
|---|---|
| Build 10 draft/example Mingla pages | Build 10 draft activation motions for high-fit prospects |
| Publish first 8-10 real business pages/events/offers | Activate first 8-10 real business motions |
| Run 3 business page distribution pilots | Run 3 business activation distribution pilots |
| Draft/example page builds | Draft activation builds |

Add missing Sprint 1 cards:

- Define activation object taxonomy and ClickUp routing rules.
- Create real Supply CRM cards for Triangle + Lagos A-tier leads.
- Create real Experiment cards for the first 5 business message tests.
- Create first weekly dashboard / Friday review card.
- Convert all sample execution-folder rows into usable templates or archive them.

## Folder 02: Supply Pipeline

### List: Supply CRM

Role:

One card per business, venue, organizer, promoter, creator-host, trip operator, experience operator, or partner prospect.

Status flow:

To do -> Prospect -> Contacted -> Meeting Set -> Onboarded -> Live -> Active -> At Risk / Churned / Complete.

This is the relationship system of record.

### What Belongs Here

- A restaurant with a slow-night opportunity.
- A nightlife venue with an event.
- A promoter with recurring programming.
- A creator who could host a guide/event.
- A trip or activity operator.
- A local newsletter, tourism board, campus group, or strategic partner.

### What Does Not Belong Here

- Daily touch counts.
- Social post tasks.
- Asset production tasks.
- Product bugs.
- Spend rows.
- Generic market research without a named prospect.

### Required Fields

Every real Supply CRM card should carry:

- City.
- Segment.
- ICP tier.
- Lead score.
- Source URL.
- Instagram URL.
- Website URL.
- Contact name.
- Contact role.
- Current workaround.
- Upcoming moment.
- Pain signal.
- Personalization token.
- First ask.
- Suggested channel.
- Last touch date.
- Next action date.
- Next action type.
- Outreach step.
- Activation motion.
- Linked Experiment.
- Linked Creator Onboarding card when they say yes.
- Live activation URL.
- Result metric.

### Supply CRM Handoffs

When status becomes Contacted:

- Create or update Activity Log row for that day's touches.

When status becomes Meeting Set:

- Add meeting notes and next action.

When status becomes Onboarded:

- Create Creator Onboarding card.
- Link the Supply CRM card.
- Keep Supply CRM owner responsible for relationship follow-up.

When status becomes Live:

- Add live URL.
- Create proof capture task if there is any demand signal.

When status becomes Active:

- Log repeat publish, repeat share, buyer/lead evidence, or partner behavior.

### Immediate Gap

Only sample Supply CRM cards exist. Sprint 1 cannot execute until real prospects from Triangle and Lagos are created here.

- Revenue/supply/demand impact.

## Weekly Rituals

### Monday: Sprint Control

Output:

- Confirm active sprint cards.
- Confirm target city/ICP/channel/message.
- Create execution cards in the right lists.
- Assign owners.
- Confirm Friday metrics.

Required linked cards:

- Supply CRM lead cards.
- Experiment cards.
- Content Production cards.
- Activity Log template for the week.
- Creator Onboarding cards for existing yeses.

### Tuesday-Thursday: Execution

Output:

- Daily touches logged.
- Replies and objections captured.
- Assets requested.
- Content shipped.
- Activation setup progressed.
- Spend logged.
- Standups completed.

### Friday: Review And Redirect

Output:

- Experiment verdicts: scale, iterate, kill.
- Supply funnel review.
- Activated supply count.
- Demand signal count.
- Proof captured.
- Product blockers.
- Spend review.
- Next-week wedge.

Friday questions:

- Which ICP moved fastest?
- Which message got qualified replies?
- Which channel produced setup conversations?
- Which activation motion actually got shared?
- Which operator friction repeated?
- Which content helped sales or Explorer demand?
- What must be killed?
- What deserves scale?

## Recommended ClickUp Governance

### One Card Per Thing

- Sprint Board: one commitment.
- Supply CRM: one relationship.
- Experiment: one hypothesis.
- Influencer CRM: one creator/partner relationship.
- Activity Log: one person/day.
- Creator Onboarding: one accepted activation.
- Content Production: one asset.
- Product Growth Request: one evidence-backed product issue.
- Spend Ledger: one spend row.
- Standup: one person/day or one review.

### Required Linking Discipline

Every card should point backward and forward:

- Backward: what goal/sprint/experiment caused this?
- Forward: what result or next system does this feed?

### Kill Rules

Kill or pause work when:

- No ICP is named.
- No activation motion is named.
- No CTA is named.
- No tracking path exists.
- No next action date exists.
- The work cannot affect supply, demand, proof, retention, revenue, or learning.

## Final Structure Recommendation

Keep the six folders.

Do not add a seventh folder yet.

Add the missing execution cards and rewrite the loose language.

The system should operate like this:

Sprint Board decides the week.

Supply Pipeline finds and advances operators.

Demand & Outreach tests messages, channels, creators, and daily activity.

Content Engine fulfills activations and creates assets only when pulled by a test, onboarding, or proof need.

Ops catches spend and product friction.

Feedback & Re-direct forces daily visibility and Friday decisions.

This gives Mingla one growth machine instead of six disconnected boards.
