# Mingla Growth — Team SOPs (how to use the system)

> One page per person. Lean v1 ClickUp = 9 lists. The rule for everyone: **if it's not in ClickUp it didn't
> happen, every task has one owner + a next action, and nothing is "Done" without Proof + an Outcome number.**
> AI drafts; **humans close.** The only metrics that count: creators onboarded · listings published · RSVPs/tickets.

---

## Everyone — the daily 3 (takes 5 min)
1. **Open your Standup card**, fill `Shipped / Blocked / Need`. If stuck, set `Feedback Status = needs_attention`.
2. **Work your lane's list** (below). Move cards forward; log the number you produced in `Outcome #`; attach `Proof`.
3. **Set a Next Action date** on anything still open. A card with no next action is dead.

## Seth — Head of Product & Marketing (Owner)
- **Home:** the Sprint Board + the Decision Log doc.
- **Daily:** scan `Needs Review`; approve/reject content + experiments; clear escalations the AI loop sends you.
- **Weekly:** Mon set the one Sprint Goal + monthly theme; Fri run the retro, make Scale/Kill calls, log decisions.
- **You don't produce** — you set direction, approve, and unblock. Final approver on every blog before publish.
- **Done means:** the week has one clear number, blockers are cleared, and Friday's decisions are logged.

## Aaron — Head of Growth (Quarterback)
- **Home:** Sprint Board + Experiments + Content Production.
- **Daily:** check pipeline movement; make sure everyone has a card in `In Progress`; brief content needed today.
- **Weekly:** Mon assign each person's goal list + lock 5 hypotheses; Wed kill losers + reallocate; Fri verdicts.
- **You own the number** (CAC + activation) and commission all blogs/pages/social (you brief, AI writes).
- **Done means:** every person has clear cards, 5 experiments ran, winners scaled, losers killed.

## Sales Support — Supply Hunter / SDR (the #1 job)
- **Home:** Supply CRM + Activity Log.
- **Daily:** source new leads → add as Supply CRM cards; contact your quota; log counts in your Activity Log card
  (calls/emails/DMs/replies/meetings). Every lead gets a Next Action date.
- **Outreach order:** venues → call, then email, then IG. Creators → IG DM, then WhatsApp, then call after interest.
- **When someone says yes:** create a Creator Onboarding card + hand off assets to Creator Success flow.
- **Done means:** quota of contacts hit (logged), and X venues/creators moved toward LIVE this week.

## Community & Social Manager
- **Home:** Activity Log (social/community) + Experiments.
- **Daily:** post the day's variants (each with a tracked link tied to a hypothesis); work Raleigh/Lagos community
  groups (Telegram/WhatsApp); reply to DMs/comments; log counts (posts/DMs/communities/follower delta).
- **Weekly:** report which messages got replies → feed the Messaging Baseline.
- **Done means:** posts shipped with tracked links, communities engaged, counts logged with Proof (screenshots).

## Brand Marketer — On-camera + Influencers
- **Home:** Influencer CRM + Content Production.
- **Daily:** work the Influencer CRM (contact → follow-up → book); shoot on-camera content per the week's briefs;
  log touches on each influencer card.
- **Weekly:** deliver founder/creator videos for the active hypotheses; follow up every un-replied influencer >3 days.
- **Done means:** N influencers contacted (logged), videos delivered with URLs, bookings moving.

## Graphics & Motion Designer
- **Home:** Content Production (your assigned cards).
- **Daily:** pull cards in `Design needed`; produce the assets for the week's hypotheses; attach the asset URL as Proof.
- **Rule:** never start from blank — use the AI design-inspiration brief from the AI Playbooks doc first.
- **Done means:** the card has the final asset URL attached and is moved to the next status.

## Video Editor
- **Home:** Content Production (video cards).
- **Daily:** cut Brand Marketer's footage into the short-form variants on your cards; attach the video URL as Proof.
- **Weekly:** deliver the spotlight/launch clips for live creators.

**Product blocker (when the product gets in the way):** don't complain in chat — create a Product Growth Requests
card **with evidence** ("7 creators couldn't publish because ticket setup confused them"), and it routes to engineering.

**Spend logging (every dollar → the Spend Ledger):** one list is the single source of truth for ad + influencer spend.
- **Ads (Aaron) — DAILY, $5/day cap per campaign:** each active ad campaign gets ONE Spend Ledger row per day
  (`Type=Ads`, Channel, `Planned=$5`); at day's end set `Amount $` to actual + flip to `Paid`. Cap is **$5/day per
  active ad campaign/channel** — if a campaign's daily actual exceeds $5, that's overspend (visible in `Planned vs Paid`),
  pause or justify. Link each row to its Experiment card.
- **Influencers (Brand Marketer / Sales):** when an influencer is booked, add a row `Type=Influencer` with the deal
  amount, linked to that Influencer card; flip to `Paid` when paid. Mirror the amount on the influencer card's `Deal Terms/$`.
- **Why:** CPL/CAC = ledger spend (by channel/type) ÷ outcomes. A live paid Experiment or booked influencer with **no
  ledger row** = the "CPL unknown" flag — so no spend is ever invisible. Per-influencer and per-channel ROI both fall out of this.
- **See it:** Spend Ledger views — **By Channel**, **By Type (Ads vs Influencer)**, **This Month**, **Planned vs Paid**.
