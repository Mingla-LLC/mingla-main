# Mingla — 90-Day Supply-Acquisition Plan (GTM motion)

> The go-to-market *motion* that the [GROWTH_OS](GROWTH_OS.md) operating rhythm and the
> [GROWTH_TEAM_CLICKUP_SYSTEM](GROWTH_TEAM_CLICKUP_SYSTEM.md) tooling exist to run. Ingested from a
> ChatGPT strategy session (2026-06-18) + reconciled by the orchestrator against Mingla's real state.
> Lifecycle/status: World Map META-ORCH-1160.

## 1. The core thesis (the reframe)

Mingla already has "make the product" and "make the content" covered. **The missing piece is supply-acquisition
HUNTERS** — operators whose only job is to get hosts, venues, promoters, creators, and trip organizers onboarded
*this week*. Not more marketers, not more awareness, not more strategy. People who close supply.

**The metric that matters is not** prompts written / ideas generated / content made / "awareness." **It is:**
creators contacted → creators onboarded → events/trips/experiences published → RSVPs/tickets generated → repeat
creators. AI makes the team faster; **humans close.** AI must never become the place work goes to *feel* productive.

This sharpens (does not replace) our supply-first read: instrument lightly, but the bottleneck right now is
**acquisition labor + concierge onboarding**, not measurement. Defer the heavy warehouse/CPL machinery until live
supply exists.

## 2. Markets & beachhead

Six markets: **Lagos · DC · Raleigh · Cary · Durham · New York.** Do NOT attack all six equally.
**Beachheads first: Lagos + the Triangle (Raleigh/Cary/Durham).** Lagos = dense culture/nightlife/trips/creators;
Triangle = controllable, relationship-buildable, and already our only real supply cluster (12 live offerings).
DC + NY are valuable but noisy → enter once the playbook works.

## 4. The concierge-onboarding motion (the unlock)

Do NOT ask early creators to "try the platform." Ask for their *next event* and do the work for them:
> "Send us your flyer, event details, ticket tiers, and social links — we'll build the Mingla page and the promo pack."

Every onboarded creator gets a **promo pack**: IG caption · story copy · WhatsApp broadcast copy · short email/SMS ·
3 talking points · 1 reel script · a trackable link. No page is published silently.

## 5. The 90-day plan (6 phases) + scorecard

| Phase | Days | Objective |
|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 Build the machine | 1–15 | Set up the ClickUp OS, lead lists per city, start outbound |
| 2 Concierge acquisition | 16–30 | Publish the first wave of listings (concierge, not self-serve) |
| 3 City launch campaigns | 31–45 | Promote live inventory; city "what to do this weekend" content; small paid tests *where supply exists* |
| 4 Partnerships & distribution | 46–60 | Channels that bring many creators/attendees at once (universities, diaspora, newsletters, venues) |
| 5 Productize winners | 61–75 | Turn learning into playbooks; send the real product blockers to engineering |
| 6 Scale best city/segment | 76–90 | Double down on the winning city + creator type + channel; don't spread |

## 8. AI Playbooks library (reusable prompts — lives as a ClickUp Doc)

A single doc, `Mingla Growth AI Playbooks`, with one reusable prompt per job: **Lead research/scoring · Outreach
(IG/WhatsApp/email + 2 follow-ups + call script) · Objection handling · Creator onboarding pack (titles, descriptions,
vibe tags, captions, broadcast copy) · Event/Trip/Venue pitch · City launch content · Founder/creator video scripts ·
Design inspiration brief · Weekly growth report · Product-growth brief (turn a GTM blocker into an engineering issue).**
Principle: AI drafts, researches, organizes; a human verifies, sends, and closes.

## 9. Coordination rules (non-negotiable)

1. **ClickUp is the source of truth** — if it's not in ClickUp, it doesn't exist. No WhatsApp-only tasks.
2. **One owner per task** — collaborators help; one person is accountable.
3. **Every lead has a next-action date** — a lead without one is dead.
4. **Every published listing gets a promo pack** — never publish silently.
5. **Every product request carries evidence** — "7 creators failed to publish because ticket setup confused them,"
   not "make onboarding better." Routes via the **Product Growth Requests** list → engineering.

## 10. How this reconciles with our existing system (what we adopt / adapt / defer)

- **ADOPT:** the supply-acquisition-hunter framing; concierge onboarding + promo packs; the per-city lead lists; the
  Creator-Onboarding (post-yes) pipeline; the Product-Growth-Requests growth→engineering bridge; the AI Playbooks doc;
  the Google Voice + WhatsApp Business comms stack; the "AI assists, humans close" guardrail (it reinforces our
  Proof+Outcome Definition-of-Done).
- **ADAPT:** our [GROWTH_TEAM_CLICKUP_SYSTEM](GROWTH_TEAM_CLICKUP_SYSTEM.md) already has Sprint Board, Experiments,
  Content Engine, Spend, Automation Health, the feedback loop. We **reframe "Venue CRM" → "Supply CRM"** (creators +
  venues + promoters + trip hosts, not just venues) and **add four lists**: City Launches (per-city), Creator
  Onboarding, Product Growth Requests, + the AI Playbooks doc. (Folded into that doc's §9B.)
- **DEFER (matches the thesis):** the RudderStack→BigQuery→Metabase warehouse + CPL/CAC depth + heavy awareness/paid.
  Lightweight tracking (UTMs, Mixpanel, ClickUp outcomes) now; the warehouse only once there's live supply to measure.
- **DON'T LOSE:** our discipline that nothing is "Done" without Proof + an Outcome number — it's the exact antidote to
  the thesis's own warning about AI-as-productivity-theater.
