## Positioning Statement

For organisers and hospitality brands who want real people through the door, Mingla Business is a mobile-first event and growth operating system that helps them create, sell, share, operate, and eventually market experiences into Mingla's consumer demand graph.

Unlike standalone ticketing or ad tools, Mingla connects the supply-side workflow to a consumer app built around vibe-matching, social planning, and local discovery.

## Consumer Positioning

For couples, friend groups, and people new to a city who are tired of doing the same things, Mingla is a vibe-based discovery and planning app that turns "what should we do?" into a confident plan.

Enemy: the group chat that goes nowhere, stale date ideas, and generic local search.
