## Extracted Product Claims

- Mingla is best framed as AI-powered social discovery and hospitality growth.
- Consumer: vibe-matching app for places, plans, and people.
- Business: AI growth OS for venues and experience brands.
- Larger opportunity expands beyond event organisers into restaurants, bars, nightlife, activity venues, galleries, pop-ups, workshops, and wellness.
- Business expansion should include menus, private parties, repeat visits, campaigns, feedback, reservations/booking integrations, and AI outreach.

## Extracted GTM / Positioning Claims

- Strong combined positioning: Mingla matches people to places, menus, events, and parties that fit their vibe, then gives businesses AI tools to convert demand into revenue.
- Against 222: Mingla can be broader, self-serve, and always-on rather than controlled matched-group programming.
- Against Eventbrite/Posh-style alternatives: Mingla can combine ticketing/commerce with taste graph and social planning.
- "Pay for customers, not impressions" is a compelling business outcome frame.
