## Extracted Product Claims

- Marketing Hub has two tracks: Marketing Blasts and Mingla-Managed Ads.
- Track 1 reaches existing contacts via email/SMS/RCS.
- Track 2 acquires new customers via paid ads, starting with Meta.
- Consent/contact verification are the foundation and must be legally defensible.
- Marketing Hub becomes the mechanical layer that Mingla Brain automates later.

## Extracted GTM / Positioning Claims

- Brand promise: reach customers who already bought and find new ones without leaving Mingla.
- Strong PMM frame: "follow up with the people who already showed up."
- Compliance and consent should be positioned as trust, not friction.
