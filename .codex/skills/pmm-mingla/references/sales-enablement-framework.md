# Sales Enablement Framework

Sales enablement assets should help sales answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. Why should they care?
3. What pain does it solve?
4. Why are we different?
5. Why now?
6. How do I demo it?
7. What objections will I hear?
8. What proof can I use?
9. What should I send after the call?

## Sales One-Pager

Use:

```markdown
# Sales One-Pager: [Product/Feature]
## What it is
## Who it is for
## Pain it solves
## Why now
## Key benefits
## Differentiators
## Discovery questions
## Demo moments
## Common objections
## Recommended talk track
## Proof points
## Customer follow-up blurb
```

## Battlecards

Use:

```markdown
# Battlecard: [Competitor/Alternative]
## Competitor summary
## When we see them
## Their strengths
## Their weaknesses
## Our winning narrative
## Key differentiators
## Landmines to set
## Objection handling
## Discovery questions
## Talk track
## Proof points
## Do not say
```

Never trash competitors. Win with clarity.

## Objection Handling

Use:

| Objection | What the customer is really asking | Response | Proof point | Follow-up question |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Responses should acknowledge, reframe, prove, and ask a next question.

## Customer Interview Plan

Use:

```markdown
## Research objective
## Target participants
## Hypotheses
## Questions
## What to listen for
## Red flags
## Synthesis framework
## Expected decisions after research
```

Ask questions that uncover behavior, not opinions. Prefer "Walk me through the last time this problem happened" over "Would you use this?"

## Jobs To Be Done

Use:

```markdown
## Job
When [situation],
I want to [motivation],
so I can [desired outcome].
## Current workaround
## Trigger
## Desired progress
## Barriers
## Success criteria
## Emotional stakes
## Business stakes
```

## Metrics

KPI trees should include North Star metric, input metrics, activation metrics, engagement metrics, retention metrics, revenue metrics, quality metrics, and guardrail metrics.

For experiments:

```markdown
## Hypothesis
## Audience
## Change
## Primary metric
## Secondary metrics
## Guardrails
## Minimum success threshold
## Duration
## Decision rule
```
