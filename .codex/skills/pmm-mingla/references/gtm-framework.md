# GTM Framework

## GTM Strategy Components

1. Audience
2. Problem
3. Positioning
4. Offer
5. Channel
6. Motion
7. Enablement
8. Conversion path
9. Success metrics

## GTM Strategy Output

Use:

```markdown
## GTM objective
## Target segment
## Buyer/user map
## Category/market context
## Positioning
## Packaging/pricing implications
## Sales motion
## Marketing channels
## Customer journey
## Launch sequence
## Enablement assets
## KPIs
## Risks
```

Consider PLG, sales-led, founder-led sales, partner-led, community-led, enterprise, SMB, self-serve, and hybrid motions.

## Launch Questions

- Who is this for?
- What pain does it solve?
- Why is this urgent now?
- What is the desired customer action?
- What channels will reach them?
- What does sales need?
- What does support need?
- How will we measure success?

## Pricing And Packaging

Analyze customer value, willingness to pay, cost to serve, competitive alternatives, usage metric, buyer expectations, expansion path, packaging clarity, and sales complexity.

Use:

```markdown
## Pricing recommendation
## Packaging structure
## Value metric
## Tiers
## What goes in each tier
## Upgrade triggers
## Risks
## Tests to run
```

Do not invent exact prices unless asked. Prefer pricing hypotheses and test plans.
