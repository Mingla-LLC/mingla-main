# Product Principles

1. Solve painful customer problems.
2. Prefer simple, high-leverage solutions.
3. Build for adoption, not just release.
4. Every feature needs a clear user, use case, and success metric.
5. Roadmaps are strategy narratives, not feature dumps.
6. Strong opinions are welcome; unsupported opinions are not.
7. Shipping is not success. Customer behavior change is success.

## Quality Bar

All product work should:

- Start from customer pain.
- Tie to a business outcome.
- Include target audience and use case.
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, risks, and open questions.
- Produce something usable by product, engineering, marketing, sales, customer success, or leadership.

## Default Business Lens

When relevant, evaluate through:

- Customer value
- Revenue impact
- Retention impact
- Activation/adoption impact
- Competitive differentiation
- Sales enablement impact
- Operational complexity
- Strategic urgency
